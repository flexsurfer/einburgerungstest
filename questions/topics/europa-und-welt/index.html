Question 173

Die Bundesrepublik Deutschland ist ein Gründungsmitglied …

The Federal Republic of Germany is a founding member of …

  1. des Nordatlantikpakts (NATO).the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO).
  2. der Vereinten Nationen (VN).the United Nations (UN).
  3. der Europäischen Union (EU).the European Union (EU).
  4. des Warschauer Pakts.the Warsaw Pact.
Correct answer and explanation

C. der Europäischen Union (EU).

the European Union (EU).

The Federal Republic of Germany is among the founding members of the European Communities, from which the European Union later emerged. It was not a founding member of NATO or the United Nations.

Question 221

Deutschland ist Mitglied des Schengener Abkommens. Was bedeutet das?

Germany is a member of the Schengen Agreement. What does that mean?

  1. Deutsche können in viele Länder Europas ohne Passkontrolle reisen.Germans can travel to many European countries without passport checks.
  2. Alle Menschen können ohne Personenkontrolle in Deutschland einreisen.All people can enter Germany without identity checks.
  3. Deutsche können ohne Passkontrolle in jedes Land reisen.Germans can travel to every country without passport checks.
  4. Deutsche können in jedem Land mit dem Euro bezahlen.Germans can pay with the euro in every country.
Correct answer and explanation

A. Deutsche können in viele Länder Europas ohne Passkontrolle reisen.

Germans can travel to many European countries without passport checks.

The Schengen Agreement allows travel between many European countries without routine passport checks at internal borders. Therefore, Germans can travel to many European countries without passport checks.

Question 231

Was bedeutet der Begriff "europäische Integration"?

What does the term "European integration" mean?

  1. Damit sind amerikanische Einwanderinnen und Einwanderer in Europa gemeint.It refers to American immigrants in Europe.
  2. Der Begriff meint den Einwanderungsstopp nach Europa.The term means stopping immigration to Europe.
  3. Damit sind europäische Auswanderinnen und Auswanderer in den USA gemeint.It refers to European emigrants in the USA.
  4. Der Begriff meint den Zusammenschluss europäischer Staaten zur EU.The term means the unification of European states into the EU.
Correct answer and explanation

D. Der Begriff meint den Zusammenschluss europäischer Staaten zur EU.

The term means the unification of European states into the EU.

European integration means that European states cooperate more and more closely. An important result of this development is the European Union.

Question 237

Was war der Inhalt der "Römischen Verträge"?

What was the content of the "Treaties of Rome"?

  1. Beitritt Deutschlands zur NATOGermany's accession to NATO
  2. Gründung der Europäischen Wirtschaftsgemeinschaft (EWG)the establishment of the European Economic Community (EEC)
  3. Verpflichtung Deutschlands zu ReparationsleistungenGermany's obligation to pay reparations
  4. Festlegung der Oder-Neiße-Linie als Ostgrenzethe establishment of the Oder-Neisse line as the eastern border
Correct answer and explanation

B. Gründung der Europäischen Wirtschaftsgemeinschaft (EWG)

the establishment of the European Economic Community (EEC)

The Treaties of Rome of 1957 established, among other things, the European Economic Community, abbreviated EEC. It was an important step toward today's European Union.
