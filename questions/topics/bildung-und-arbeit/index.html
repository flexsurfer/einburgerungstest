Question 244

Welchen Schulabschluss braucht man normalerweise, um an einer Universität in Deutschland ein Studium zu beginnen?

What school-leaving qualification do you normally need to begin studying at a university in Germany?

  1. das Abiturthe Abitur
  2. ein Diploma diploma
  3. die Prokuracommercial power of attorney
  4. eine Gesellenprüfunga journeyman's examination
Correct answer and explanation

A. das Abitur

the Abitur

To study at a university, you normally need the Abitur or a comparable higher education entrance qualification. The Abitur gives access to many degree programs.

Question 250

In Deutschland hat man die besten Chancen auf einen gut bezahlten Arbeitsplatz, wenn man …

In Germany, you have the best chances of getting a well-paid job if you …

  1. katholisch ist.are Catholic.
  2. gut ausgebildet ist.are well educated and trained.
  3. eine Frau ist.are a woman.
  4. Mitglied einer Partei ist.are a member of a political party.
Correct answer and explanation

B. gut ausgebildet ist.

are well educated and trained.

Good education and professional qualifications improve the chances of getting a well-paid job. Religion, gender, or party membership must not be a requirement for this.

an evening grammar school.

The Career Information Center (BIZ) at the Federal Employment Agency in Germany helps with …

  3. Steuererklärung.tax returns.

finding an apprenticeship.

The Career Information Center, or BIZ for short, provides information about occupations, training, and work. For example, it helps with finding an apprenticeship.

Question 260

In Deutschland hat ein Kind in der Schule …

In Germany, a child at school has …

  1. Recht auf unbegrenzte Freizeit.the right to unlimited free time.
  2. Wahlfreiheit für alle Fächer.freedom to choose all subjects.
  3. Anspruch auf Schulgeld.a claim to school fees.
  4. Anwesenheitspflicht.compulsory attendance.
Correct answer and explanation

D. Anwesenheitspflicht.

compulsory attendance.

Children in Germany must attend school regularly. That is why school attendance is compulsory.

Question 284

Was man für die Arbeit können muss, ändert sich in Zukunft sehr schnell. Was kann man tun?

What you need to be able to do for work will change very quickly in the future. What can you do?

  1. Es ist egal, was man lernt.It does not matter what you learn.
  2. Erwachsene müssen auch nach der Ausbildung immer weiter lernen.Adults must continue learning even after completing their training.
  3. Kinder lernen in der Schule alles, was im Beruf wichtig ist. Nach der Schule muss man nicht weiter lernen.Children learn everything at school that is important for work. After school, you do not need to continue learning.
  4. Alle müssen früher aufhören zu arbeiten, weil sich alles ändert.Everyone must stop working earlier because everything is changing.
Correct answer and explanation

B. Erwachsene müssen auch nach der Ausbildung immer weiter lernen.

Adults must continue learning even after completing their training.

Occupations and requirements are constantly changing. That is why it is important for adults to continue learning and training even after completing their initial training.

Question 285

Frau Frost arbeitet als fest angestellte Mitarbeiterin in einem Büro. Was muss sie nicht von ihrem Gehalt bezahlen?

Ms. Frost works as a permanently employed office worker. What does she not have to pay from her salary?

  1. Lohnsteuerwage tax
  2. Beiträge zur Arbeitslosenversicherungunemployment insurance contributions
  3. Beiträge zur Renten- und Krankenversicherungpension and health insurance contributions
  4. Umsatzsteuervalue-added tax
Correct answer and explanation

D. Umsatzsteuer

value-added tax

A permanently employed person pays, for example, wage tax and social insurance contributions from their salary. Value-added tax, however, is not deducted directly from salary.

Question 287

Sie möchten bei einer Firma in Deutschland ihr Arbeitsverhältnis beenden. Was müssen Sie beachten?

You want to end your employment relationship with a company in Germany. What do you have to take into account?

  1. die Gehaltszahlungensalary payments
  2. die Arbeitszeitworking hours
  3. die Kündigungsfristthe notice period
  4. die Versicherungspflichtthe insurance obligation
Correct answer and explanation

C. die Kündigungsfrist

the notice period

Anyone who wants to end their employment relationship must observe the applicable notice period. It determines how long the employment relationship continues after notice is given.
