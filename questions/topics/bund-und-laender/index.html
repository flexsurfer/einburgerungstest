Federal and state government

Germany has 16 federal states. This topic helps you distinguish federal, state and local government. Learn the general terms first, then study the ten additional questions for your own federal state.

English translations help you learn. The official examination is in German.

Question 24

Wie viele Bundesländer hat die Bundesrepublik Deutschland?

How many federal states does the Federal Republic of Germany have?

  1. 1414
  2. 1515
  3. 1616
  4. 1717
Correct answer and explanation

C. 16

16

Germany consists of 16 federal states. These include, for example, Bavaria, Brandenburg, North Rhine-Westphalia, and Saxony.

  2. Nordrhein-WestfalenNorth Rhine-Westphalia
  3. Mecklenburg-VorpommernMecklenburg-Western Pomerania

Correct answer and explanation

A. Elsass-Lothringen

Alsace-Lorraine

Alsace-Lorraine is not a German federal state. North Rhine-Westphalia, Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, and Saxony-Anhalt, by contrast, are among the 16 federal states.

Question 39

Was hat jedes deutsche Bundesland?

What does every German federal state have?

  1. eine eigene Außenministerin / einen eigenen Außenministerits own foreign minister
  2. eine eigene Währungits own currency
  3. eine eigene Armeeits own army
  4. eine eigene Regierungits own government
Correct answer and explanation

D. eine eigene Regierung

its own government

Every German federal state has its own state government. By contrast, not every federal state has its own currency, army, or foreign ministry.

Who determines school policy in Germany?

  1. die Lehrer und Lehrerinnenteachers
  2. die Bundesländerthe federal states
  3. das Familienministeriumthe Family Ministry
  4. die Universitätenuniversities
Correct answer and explanation

B. die Bundesländer

the federal states

In Germany, school policy is primarily the responsibility of the federal states. Therefore, rules and school systems can differ from one state to another.

Who makes up the German Bundesrat?

  1. die Abgeordneten des Bundestagesthe members of the Bundestag
  2. die Ministerinnen und Minister der Bundesregierungthe ministers of the federal government
  3. die Regierungsvertreter der Bundesländerthe government representatives of the federal states

Question 91

In Deutschland kann ein Regierungswechsel in einem Bundesland Auswirkungen auf die Bundespolitik haben. Das Regieren wird …

In Germany, a change of government in a federal state can affect federal politics. Governing becomes ...

  1. schwieriger, wenn sich dadurch die Mehrheit im Bundestag ändert.more difficult if this changes the majority in the Bundestag.
  2. leichter, wenn dadurch neue Parteien in den Bundesrat kommen.easier if new parties thereby enter the Bundesrat.
  3. schwieriger, wenn dadurch die Mehrheit im Bundesrat verändert wird.more difficult if this changes the majority in the Bundesrat.
  4. leichter, wenn es sich um ein reiches Bundesland handelt.easier if it is a wealthy federal state.
Correct answer and explanation

C. schwieriger, wenn dadurch die Mehrheit im Bundesrat verändert wird.

more difficult if this changes the majority in the Bundesrat.

A change of government in a federal state can alter the balance of majorities in the Bundesrat. This can make it more difficult for the federal government to pass certain laws.
