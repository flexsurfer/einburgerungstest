Question 33

Welche Aussage ist richtig? In Deutschland …

Which statement is correct? In Germany …

  1. sind Staat und Religionsgemeinschaften voneinander getrennt.the state and religious communities are separate from one another.
  2. bilden die Religionsgemeinschaften den Staat.religious communities form the state.
  3. ist der Staat abhängig von den Religionsgemeinschaften.the state is dependent on religious communities.
  4. bilden Staat und Religionsgemeinschaften eine Einheit.the state and religious communities form a single unit.
Correct answer and explanation

A. sind Staat und Religionsgemeinschaften voneinander getrennt.

the state and religious communities are separate from one another.

The state and religious communities are not identical in Germany. The state is religiously neutral while also protecting freedom of religion.

With which words does the German national anthem begin?

  2. Einigkeit und Recht und Freiheit …Unity and justice and freedom …
  3. Freude schöner Götterfunken …Joy, beautiful spark of divinity …
  4. Deutschland einig Vaterland …Germany, united fatherland …
Correct answer and explanation

B. Einigkeit und Recht und Freiheit …

Unity and justice and freedom …

The German national anthem begins with the words 'Unity and justice and freedom'. These words are at the beginning of the stanza used today.

  1. bunte Eier versteckenhiding colorful eggs

  3. sich mit Masken und Kostümen verkleidendressing up with masks and costumes

Correct answer and explanation

decorating a Christmas tree

A typical Christmas custom in Germany is a decorated Christmas tree. Many families put one up at home during Advent or the Christmas season.

Question 291

Warum muss man in Deutschland bei der Steuererklärung aufschreiben, ob man zu einer Kirche gehört oder nicht? Weil …

Why do you have to state on your tax return in Germany whether or not you belong to a church? Because …

  1. es eine Kirchensteuer gibt, die an die Einkommen- und Lohnsteuer geknüpft ist.there is a church tax linked to income tax and wage tax.
  2. das für die Statistik in Deutschland wichtig ist.it is important for statistics in Germany.
  3. man mehr Steuern zahlen muss, wenn man nicht zu einer Kirche gehört.you have to pay more taxes if you do not belong to a church.
  4. die Kirche für die Steuererklärung verantwortlich ist.the church is responsible for the tax return.
Correct answer and explanation

A. es eine Kirchensteuer gibt, die an die Einkommen- und Lohnsteuer geknüpft ist.

there is a church tax linked to income tax and wage tax.

Members of certain churches in Germany pay church tax. That is why the tax return asks whether you belong to a corresponding religious community.

Question 292

Die Menschen in Deutschland leben nach dem Grundsatz der religiösen Toleranz. Was bedeutet das?

People in Germany live according to the principle of religious tolerance. What does that mean?

  1. Es dürfen keine Moscheen gebaut werden.No mosques may be built.
  2. Alle Menschen glauben an Gott.Everyone believes in God.
  3. Jeder kann glauben, was er möchte.Everyone can believe what they want.
  4. Der Staat entscheidet, an welchen Gott die Menschen glauben.The state decides which god people believe in.
Correct answer and explanation

C. Jeder kann glauben, was er möchte.

Everyone can believe what they want.

Religious tolerance means that people may freely choose and practice their faith. In other words, people can believe what they want as long as they obey the law.
