  2. Bild 2Image 2
  3. Bild 3Image 3

Question 3 · Bremen

Für wie viele Jahre wird das Landesparlament in Bremen gewählt?

For how many years is the state parliament in Bremen elected?

  1. 33
  2. 44
  3. 55
  4. 66
Correct answer and explanation

B. 4

4

The correct answer is 4 years. The state parliament in Bremen is elected for four years. After that, a new election is normally held.

Question 4 · Bremen

Ab welchem Alter darf man in Bremen bei den Wahlen zur Bürgerschaft (Landtag) wählen?

From what age can you vote in elections to the Bürgerschaft (state parliament) in Bremen?

  1. 1414
  2. 1616
  3. 1818
  4. 2020
Correct answer and explanation

B. 16

16

The correct answer is 16. In Bremen you can vote in this election from the age of 16. The voting age can vary depending on the federal state and the type of election.

Question 6 · Bremen

Where can you get information about political topics in Bremen?

  1. beim Ordnungsamt der Gemeindeat the municipal public order office
  2. bei der Landeszentrale für politische Bildungat the State Agency for Civic Education
  3. bei den Kirchenat the churches
  4. bei der Verbraucherzentraleat the consumer advice center
Correct answer and explanation

B. bei der Landeszentrale für politische Bildung

at the State Agency for Civic Education

The correct answer is at the State Agency for Civic Education. This institution provides neutral information about politics, democracy, and social issues. For example, it offers informational materials and educational programs.

Which federal state is Bremen?

  1. 11
  2. 22
  3. 33
  4. 44

A. 1

1

Question 9 · Bremen

  2. Erste Bürgermeisterin / Erster BürgermeisterFirst Mayor
  3. Präsidentin / Präsident des SenatesPresident of the Senate
  4. Regierende Bürgermeisterin / Regierender BürgermeisterGoverning Mayor

Question 10 · Bremen
