Question 3 · Hamburg

For how many years is the state parliament in Hamburg elected?

  1. 33
  2. 44
  3. 55
  4. 66
Correct answer and explanation

C. 5

5

The correct answer is 5 years. The state parliament in Hamburg is elected for five years. After that, a new election is normally held.
