Question 1 · Schleswig-Holstein

Welches Wappen gehört zum Bundesland Schleswig-Holstein?

Which coat of arms belongs to the state of Schleswig-Holstein?

  1. Bild 1Image 1
  2. Bild 2Image 2
  3. Bild 3Image 3
  4. Bild 4Image 4
Correct answer and explanation

C. Bild 3

Image 3

The correct answer is Image 3. Schleswig-Holstein's coat of arms shows two blue lions and a silver nettle leaf. This distinctive symbol helps identify the state coat of arms.

Question 3 · Schleswig-Holstein

Für wie viele Jahre wird der Landtag in Schleswig-Holstein gewählt?

For how many years is the state parliament in Schleswig-Holstein elected?

  1. 33
  2. 44
  3. 55
  4. 66
Correct answer and explanation

C. 5

5

The correct answer is 5 years. The state parliament in Schleswig-Holstein is elected for five years. After that, a new election is normally held.

Question 6 · Schleswig-Holstein

Where can you get information about political topics in Schleswig-Holstein?

  1. bei der Verbraucherzentraleat the consumer advice center
  2. beim Ordnungsamt der Gemeindeat the municipal public order office
  3. beim / bei der Landesbeauftragten für politische Bildungat the office of the State Commissioner for Civic Education
  4. bei den Kirchenat the churches
Correct answer and explanation

C. beim / bei der Landesbeauftragten für politische Bildung

at the office of the State Commissioner for Civic Education

The correct answer is at the office of the State Commissioner for Civic Education. This institution provides neutral information about politics, democracy, and social issues. For example, it offers informational materials and educational programs.

Question 9 · Schleswig-Holstein

Wie nennt man die Regierungschefin / den Regierungschef in Schleswig-Holstein?

What is the head of government in Schleswig-Holstein called?

  1. Erste Ministerin / Erster MinisterFirst Minister
  2. Premierministerin / PremierministerPrime Minister
  3. Bürgermeisterin / BürgermeisterMayor
  4. Ministerpräsidentin / MinisterpräsidentMinister-President
Correct answer and explanation

D. Ministerpräsidentin / Ministerpräsident

Minister-President

The correct answer is Minister-President. This is the title of the office at the head of the state government in Schleswig-Holstein. The title differs in some city-states from that used in other federal states.

Question 10 · Schleswig-Holstein

Welche Ministerin / welchen Minister hat Schleswig-Holstein nicht?

Which minister does Schleswig-Holstein not have?

  1. Justizministerin / JustizministerJustice Minister
  2. Außenministerin / AußenministerForeign Minister
  3. Finanzministerin / FinanzministerFinance Minister
  4. Innenministerin / InnenministerInterior Minister
Correct answer and explanation

B. Außenministerin / Außenminister

Foreign Minister

The correct answer is Foreign Minister. Foreign policy is primarily the responsibility of the federal government. Therefore, this state-level office does not exist in Schleswig-Holstein.
