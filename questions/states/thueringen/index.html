Question 1 · Thüringen

Welches Wappen gehört zum Freistaat Thüringen?

Which coat of arms belongs to the Free State of Thuringia?

  1. Bild 1Image 1
  2. Bild 2Image 2
  3. Bild 3Image 3
  4. Bild 4Image 4
Correct answer and explanation

D. Bild 4

Image 4

The correct answer is Image 4. Thuringia's coat of arms shows a red-and-white striped lion with eight silver stars. This distinctive symbol helps identify the state coat of arms.

Wartburgkreis

Question 3 · Thüringen

Für wie viele Jahre wird der Landtag in Thüringen gewählt?

For how many years is the state parliament in Thuringia elected?

  1. 33
  2. 44
  3. 55
  4. 66
Correct answer and explanation

C. 5

5

The correct answer is 5 years. The state parliament in Thuringia is elected for five years. After that, a new election is normally held.

From what age can you vote in local elections in Thuringia?

  1. 1414
  2. 1616
  3. 1818
  4. 2020
Correct answer and explanation

B. 16

16

The correct answer is 16. In Thuringia you can vote in this election from the age of 16. The voting age can vary depending on the federal state and the type of election.

Question 6 · Thüringen

Wo können Sie sich in Thüringen über politische Themen informieren?

Where can you get information about political topics in Thuringia?

  1. bei den Kirchenat the churches
  2. bei der Verbraucherzentraleat the consumer advice center
  3. bei der Landeszentrale für politische Bildungat the State Agency for Civic Education
  4. beim Ordnungsamt der Gemeindeat the municipal public order office
Correct answer and explanation

C. bei der Landeszentrale für politische Bildung

at the State Agency for Civic Education

The correct answer is at the State Agency for Civic Education. This institution provides neutral information about politics, democracy, and social issues. For example, it offers informational materials and educational programs.

Question 9 · Thüringen

Wie nennt man die Regierungschefin / den Regierungschef in Thüringen?

What is the head of government in Thuringia called?

  1. Erste Ministerin / Erster MinisterFirst Minister
  2. Premierministerin / PremierministerPrime Minister
  3. Bürgermeisterin / BürgermeisterMayor
  4. Ministerpräsidentin / MinisterpräsidentMinister-President
Correct answer and explanation

D. Ministerpräsidentin / Ministerpräsident

Minister-President

The correct answer is Minister-President. This is the title of the office at the head of the state government in Thuringia. The title differs in some city-states from that used in other federal states.

Question 10 · Thüringen

Welche Ministerin / welchen Minister hat Thüringen nicht?

Which minister does Thuringia not have?

  1. Justizministerin / JustizministerJustice Minister
  2. Außenministerin / AußenministerForeign Minister
  3. Finanzministerin / FinanzministerFinance Minister
  4. Innenministerin / InnenministerInterior Minister
Correct answer and explanation

B. Außenministerin / Außenminister

Foreign Minister

The correct answer is Foreign Minister. Foreign policy is primarily the responsibility of the federal government. Therefore, this state-level office does not exist in Thuringia.
