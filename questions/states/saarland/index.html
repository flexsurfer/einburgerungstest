Question 1 · Saarland

Welches Wappen gehört zum Bundesland Saarland?

Which coat of arms belongs to the state of Saarland?

  1. Bild 1Image 1
  2. Bild 2Image 2
  3. Bild 3Image 3
  4. Bild 4Image 4
Correct answer and explanation

D. Bild 4

Image 4

The correct answer is Image 4. Saarland's coat of arms consists of four fields with historical symbols of the region. This distinctive symbol helps identify the state coat of arms.

Question 3 · Saarland

Für wie viele Jahre wird der Landtag des Saarlandes gewählt?

For how many years is the state parliament of Saarland elected?

  1. 33
  2. 44
  3. 55
  4. 66
Correct answer and explanation

C. 5

5

The correct answer is 5 years. The state parliament in Saarland is elected for five years. After that, a new election is normally held.

  3. bei der Verbraucherzentraleat the consumer advice center

Correct answer and explanation

B. bei der Landeszentrale für politische Bildung

at the State Agency for Civic Education

The correct answer is at the State Agency for Civic Education. This institution provides neutral information about politics, democracy, and social issues. For example, it offers informational materials and educational programs.

  2. 22

2

Question 9 · Saarland

Wie nennt man die Regierungschefin / den Regierungschef des Saarlandes?

What is the head of government of Saarland called?

  1. Erste Ministerin / Erster MinisterFirst Minister
  2. Premierministerin / PremierministerPrime Minister
  3. Bürgermeisterin / BürgermeisterMayor
  4. Ministerpräsidentin / MinisterpräsidentMinister-President
Correct answer and explanation

D. Ministerpräsidentin / Ministerpräsident

Minister-President

The correct answer is Minister-President. This is the title of the office at the head of the state government in Saarland. The title differs in some city-states from that used in other federal states.

Question 10 · Saarland

Welche Ministerin / welchen Minister hat das Saarland nicht?

Which minister does das Saarland not have?

  1. Justizministerin / JustizministerJustice Minister
  2. Außenministerin / AußenministerForeign Minister
  3. Finanzministerin / FinanzministerFinance Minister
  4. Innenministerin / InnenministerInterior Minister
Correct answer and explanation

B. Außenministerin / Außenminister

Foreign Minister

The correct answer is Foreign Minister. Foreign policy is primarily the responsibility of the federal government. Therefore, this state-level office does not exist in Saarland.
