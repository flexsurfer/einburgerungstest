  1. Bild 1Image 1
  2. Bild 2Image 2
  3. Bild 3Image 3
  4. Bild 4Image 4

D. Bild 4

Image 4

The correct answer is Image 4. The coat of arms of Saxony-Anhalt includes an eagle, a bear, and black-and-gold stripes. This distinctive symbol helps identify the state coat of arms.

For how many years is the state parliament in Saxony-Anhalt elected?

  1. 33
  2. 44
  3. 55
  4. 66

C. 5

5

The correct answer is 5 years. The state parliament in Saxony-Anhalt is elected for five years. After that, a new election is normally held.

  3. Bürgermeisterin / BürgermeisterMayor

Question 10 · Sachsen-Anhalt

  4. Innenministerin / InnenministerInterior Minister
Correct answer and explanation

The correct answer is Foreign Minister. Foreign policy is primarily the responsibility of the federal government. Therefore, this state-level office does not exist in Saxony-Anhalt.
