Question 3

Deutschland ist ein Rechtsstaat. Was ist damit gemeint?

Germany is a state governed by the rule of law. What does that mean?

  1. Alle Einwohnerinnen / Einwohner und der Staat müssen sich an die Gesetze halten.All residents and the state must obey the laws.
  2. Der Staat muss sich nicht an die Gesetze halten.The state does not have to obey the laws.
  3. Nur Deutsche müssen die Gesetze befolgen.Only Germans must obey the laws.
  4. Die Gerichte machen die Gesetze.The courts make the laws.
Correct answer and explanation

A. Alle Einwohnerinnen / Einwohner und der Staat müssen sich an die Gesetze halten.

All residents and the state must obey the laws.

A state governed by the rule of law means that both the people and the state must obey the laws. Government authorities are also not allowed to simply act against the law.

Start practice online

How to recognise the correct answer

Notice the words “and the state” in the correct answer. Public authorities also have to act within the law. Article 20(3) of the Basic Law sets out the legal constraints on the legislature, the executive and the judiciary.
