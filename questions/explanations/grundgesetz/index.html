Question 6

Wie heißt die deutsche Verfassung?

What is the German constitution called?

  1. VolksgesetzPeople's Law
  2. BundesgesetzFederal Law
  3. Deutsches GesetzGerman Law
  4. GrundgesetzBasic Law
Correct answer and explanation

D. Grundgesetz

Basic Law

Germany's constitution is called the Basic Law. It contains important rules for the state and the fundamental rights of the people.

Start practice online

How to recognise the correct answer

Verfassung is the German word for constitution. Germany's constitution is called the Grundgesetz, or Basic Law. Learn the connection between these two words: another question uses the longer phrase “constitution of the Federal Republic of Germany”.
